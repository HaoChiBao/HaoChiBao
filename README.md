## Work

**RBC Amplify**, May 2025 to August 2025. Liquidity forecasting for commercial banking. An infinite canvas for mapping cash flow, and three agents: analyzer, architect, recommender. Commercial Banking adopted it to oversee $10B+ in annual transactions. It won a $20K Industry Disruptor Prize.

**Boardy**, February 2025 to May 2025. A meeting agent that joins Zoom and Google Meet, listens, and talks back. 99% of joins succeed. Voice comes back in under 500ms.
